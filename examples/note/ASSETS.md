# Bundled image provenance

`resources/typewriter.png` is an original image generated for Bonsai Note on
September 15, 2026 with the built-in imagegen tool. It is visually similar subject
matter, not a crop of Craft's cover or the reference screenshot. It is bundled on
macOS and iOS and needs no runtime network request.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: bundled reading-note cover
> photograph. Create a wide 3:2 macro photograph of the round ivory keys of a
> vintage black mechanical typewriter, viewed diagonally from above, tightly
> cropped so keys fill the entire frame. Small worn metal rims, black letters,
> dark mechanics and warm amber highlights, muted natural light, rich blacks.
> Focus across the center, subtle depth of field. No additional objects, logos,
> captions, border or watermark. This is a standalone image asset, not a UI mockup.

The image was generated at 1536×1024 and copied unchanged into the repository.
All template previews are composed from real OCaml views. Their text and colors
are deterministic fixtures. The reading fixture uses original sample prose;
it does not transcribe or attribute that prose to Robert Pirosh.
